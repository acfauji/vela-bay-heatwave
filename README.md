An original sun-soaked open-world crime game: explore nine districts of Vela Bay on foot or by car, steal and drive eight vehicle models, outrun a five-star police response, and work five story contracts for Rook Adisa's crew. Everything — city, characters, art, sound and music — is authored for this entry.

## Run it
Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome, or serve the folder locally with `node serve.ts` and visit `http://localhost:8080`. No build step and no network requests are needed.

Controls: WASD to move/drive, mouse to aim with right-drag or Q/E to rotate the camera, Shift to sprint, Space to dodge or handbrake, F to enter/exit a car, left-click to shoot, R to reload, 1-5 for weapons, C for chase camera, M for the map, Esc to pause/save/load, and V to mute.
